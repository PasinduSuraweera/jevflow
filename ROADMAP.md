# Willowglen roadmap

JevFlow is an original autonomous village experiment. Jev provides decisions; the world, mechanics and interface are ours.

## Implemented

- Procedural 3D island with eleven places and an uneven, seeded coastline.
- Five starting residents, a resident editor and up to eight villagers.
- Needs, coins, skill, twenty-two activities, shared food, wood and goods, and short memories.
- Seasons, five weather types, opening hours, events, scenario presets and adjustable rules.
- Jev-chosen companions, friendships and a Jev village council for community projects.
- Community upgrades, milestones, follow camera, minimap and activity progress.
- Day/night lighting, seasonal colors, rain, snow, storms and ambient village animation.
- Save, load, import and export of worlds and decision histories, never including keys.
- Informational confidence with uninterrupted legal activity choices.
- Real-key server proxy, validated legal choices and visible probabilities.
- World influences, pause, speed, single decision and request budget.
- Original workbench preserved at `/lab`.
- Mocked integration and simulation tests.

## Before release

- Verify the 3D scene and controls on GPU-backed desktop and mobile browsers.
- Configure HTTPS, infrastructure logging policy and deployment rate limits for public hosting.

## Future experiments

- Replay a decision history step by step.
- Coordinated social animation and conversations shown as gestures.
- Player-built buildings and paths.
- Shared scenarios others can import from a link.

These future experiments are not implemented. Share measured, real-provider demonstrations with credit to TypeSafe as the decision provider.
